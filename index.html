Lucid Brief Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026 · Lucid Brief was called SchoolBrief until September 27, 2026

Lucid Brief ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile app from LUCID ADAPTIVE LEARNING LLC that reads the school apps a family already uses and shows what needs attention in one brief. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and how you delete it.

Lucid Brief is intended solely for use by parents and legal guardians. Children do not have accounts and do not use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Account

  • Email address and password. Used to create and sign in to your account. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Your school logins stay on your phone

We do not collect your school passwords. When you connect Infinite Campus, ParentSquare or EZSchoolPay, the login you enter is kept only in your phone's encrypted keychain. It is used on your phone to sign in to that school system, and it is never sent to our servers or to any company other than the school system itself. For Google Classroom you sign in with Google on your phone; we never see that password.

Your child's school information

Your phone reads your school accounts and sends us the facts your brief needs:

  • Child profile — the name and grade you enter, your child's school, and your district's portal address.
  • Academic records — assignments, due dates, missing work, scores, course names, teacher names and course grades.
  • School communications — messages and announcements from ParentSquare and Google Classroom.
  • Lunch account balance from EZSchoolPay.
  • Derived history — the briefs we build, a record of when each assignment appeared or changed, and score history used to draw grade trends.

Settings and subscription

  • Your preferences — time zone, grade alert line, lunch balance alert, and similar choices. Your reminder days and time stay on your phone.
  • Subscription status — which plan you have.

What we do not collect. Lucid Brief contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any advertising identifier, and there is no third-party ad network in the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To build your brief from the facts your phone sends.
  • To show grade trends and past briefs.
  • On a paid plan, to summarise school messages and pull out their dates and requests (section 5).
  • To tell you when a connection needs attention.
  • To operate your subscription and provide support.

We do not use your information, or your child's, for advertising, for marketing to anyone, to build a profile of your child for any other purpose, to train machine learning models, or for any purpose beyond running the service described here. We do not sell it.

3. How Your School Accounts Are Read

Infinite Campus, ParentSquare and EZSchoolPay do not offer a way for an outside app to request access. So your phone signs in with the login you entered, reads the same pages you would see if you signed in yourself, and sends us only the facts listed above. It does not change anything, post anything, or make payments.

Your phone checks only when you ask it to — when you pull down to refresh, or tap the reminder you set. Our servers never sign in to a school.

Google Classroom works through Google's own sign-in on your phone, and you can revoke access at any time from your Google Account permissions page without involving us.

Lucid Brief is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Columbia County School District, Infinite Campus, ParentSquare, EZSchoolPay, or Google. All product names belong to their respective owners.

4. Google User Data

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Lucid Brief requests these Google permissions, all read-only:

PermissionWhy
classroom.courses.readonlyYour child's classes
classroom.coursework.me.readonlyAssignments and due dates in those classes
classroom.announcements.readonlyTeacher announcements in the brief
classroom.student-submissions.me.readonlyWhether your child's own work is turned in, and its grade

We do not request class rosters or any other student's work. Google Classroom data is used only to build the brief shown to you. It is never transferred to anyone else, never used for advertising, never used to train models, and never read by a human except with your explicit permission when you ask us for help with a specific problem.

5. Who Else Receives Your Information

We do not sell your personal information or your child's, and we do not share it for advertising or any other company's own purposes. We use these service providers, each processing data only on our instructions:

People you choose. Nobody else sees your child's information unless you share it. In the app, Profile → Sharing lets you share one child with a co-parent, who sees everything you see and can refresh with their own school login on their own phone, or with a tutor, who sees schoolwork only — assignments, scores and class grades, not school messages or the lunch balance — as of your last refresh. Each share starts with a one-time code that you send yourself, and the person you share with sees your email address. You can stop sharing at any time; a tutor's access also ends by itself after 90 days.

Lucid Brief sends no push notifications from a server. The only notification is the reminder your phone schedules for itself, and it carries no school information.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to, or to protect the safety of a person, and we will tell you unless the law forbids it.

6. Data Security

Your school passwords never leave your phone. Everything else travels over HTTPS, and every record on our servers is protected by row-level security keyed to your account, so one family's data cannot be read by another. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users without undue delay if we ever learn of a breach involving their data.

7. Children's Privacy

Lucid Brief is for parents and legal guardians only. Children do not create accounts, do not sign in, and do not interact with the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child, and the app is not directed to children.

All information about a child in Lucid Brief is supplied by, or retrieved at the direction of, the parent or guardian who holds the account and who has consented on their child's behalf. By creating an account you confirm that you are the parent or legal guardian of the child whose information you connect, and that you are entitled to access that child's school accounts.

If you believe a child has provided us information without a parent's involvement, email support@lucidlearner.com and we will delete it promptly.

Education records

Lucid Brief acts at your direction, as a parent exercising your own right of access to your child's education records. We are not a school official and we have no agreement with your school district. We do not receive records from a school; your phone retrieves them using the access you already have, and they are shown only back to you.

9. Your Choices and Rights

  • Remove one child. Settings → Children → the bin beside the child. Their school logins leave your phone and everything we hold about them is erased.
  • Delete your account. Settings → Account → Delete account. Immediate and permanent, with nothing to email us for.
  • Disconnect a school account at any time from Settings.
  • Revoke Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Access or correct the information we hold, by asking us.

To exercise any right, or if a deletion does not work as described, contact support@lucidlearner.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. If a change materially affects what we collect or who receives it, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect, and update the date at the top of this page.
