SchoolBrief Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

SchoolBrief ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application that gathers what changed today across the school apps a family already uses, and reports it as a single daily summary. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else sees it, how long we keep it, and how you delete it.

SchoolBrief is intended solely for use by parents and legal guardians. Children do not have accounts and do not use the app.

1. Information We Collect

Account

  • Email address and password. Used to create and sign in to your account. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.

Credentials for your school portals

To retrieve your child's information, SchoolBrief signs in to your school accounts as you. To do that it stores:

  • Infinite Campus username and password
  • ParentSquare email and password
  • EZSchoolPay username and password
  • Google Classroom — no password. You authorize us through Google's own sign-in screen, and we store the resulting access and refresh tokens plus the Google email address you signed in with.

You choose which of these to connect. None is required to create an account. See section 4 for exactly how these are used.

Your child's school information

  • Child profile — the name and grade level you enter, and your school district's portal address.
  • Academic records — assignments, due dates, missing work, scores, course names, teacher names and course grades retrieved from Infinite Campus and Google Classroom.
  • School communications — announcements and messages from ParentSquare and Google Classroom.
  • Lunch account balance from EZSchoolPay.
  • Derived history — the daily briefs we generate, a record of when each assignment first appeared or changed, and score history used to draw grade trends.

Google Classroom's roster permission means we may see the names of other students enrolled in your child's courses. We use it only to identify which coursework belongs to your child. We do not store, display or process other students' information.

Device and app settings

  • Push notification token — an anonymous device identifier issued by Expo, used to deliver your daily brief.
  • Your preferences — delivery time, quiet hours, time zone, grade alert threshold and lunch balance threshold.
  • Subscription status — whether you have an active SchoolBrief+ subscription, its billing interval and renewal date.

What we do not collect. SchoolBrief contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any advertising identifier. We do not build a profile of you, and there is no third-party ad network in the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To sign in to the school services you connected and retrieve your child's data on your behalf.
  • To compare today against yesterday and generate your daily brief.
  • To send you push notifications: the daily brief, grade alerts below your chosen threshold, low lunch balance alerts, and — for SchoolBrief+ subscribers — intra-day updates and a Sunday weekly digest.
  • To display briefs, grade trends and weekly summaries inside the app.
  • To tell you when a connection has stopped working and needs to be re-authorized.
  • To operate your subscription and provide customer support.

We do not use your information, or your child's, for advertising, for marketing to you or to anyone else, to train machine learning models, or for any purpose beyond running the service described here.

3. How We Access Your School Accounts

This section is the most important one to understand before you connect an account.

Infinite Campus, ParentSquare and EZSchoolPay do not offer a way for an outside app to request access. So for those three, SchoolBrief stores the username and password you provide and uses them to log in to the service as you, from our servers, on a schedule — typically once each morning, and more often if you subscribe to SchoolBrief+. It reads the same pages you would see if you logged in yourself. It does not change anything, post anything, or make payments.

Google Classroom works differently and better: Google lets you grant access directly, so we never see or hold your Google password, and you can revoke our access at any time from your Google Account permissions page without involving us.

Deleting your account removes every stored credential immediately. You can also withdraw Google Classroom access on your own at any time, directly from Google, without involving us.

SchoolBrief is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the Columbia County School District, Infinite Campus, ParentSquare, EZSchoolPay, or Google. All product names belong to their respective owners.

SchoolBrief requests these Google permissions, all read-only:

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase, a SOC 2 Type II compliant cloud platform, in the United States. All traffic between the app, our servers and the school services travels over HTTPS.

School portal passwords and Google tokens are held in Supabase Vault, encrypted at rest with a key that is not stored alongside them, and are never written to our application database in plain text. They are decrypted only inside the server process that is performing a sync, and are never sent to your device or to anyone else.

Every record is protected by database row-level security keyed to your account, so one customer's data cannot be read by another.

No system is perfectly secure. Because a compromise of these credentials would affect your school accounts and not only SchoolBrief, we treat them as the most sensitive data we hold, and we will notify affected users without undue delay if we ever learn of a breach involving them.

We may also disclose information if we are legally required to, or to protect the safety of a person, and we will tell you unless the law forbids it.

7. Children's Privacy

SchoolBrief is for parents and legal guardians only. Children do not create accounts, do not sign in, and do not interact with the app. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from a child, and the app is not directed to children.

All information about a child in SchoolBrief — name, grade, academic records, and school communications — is supplied by, or retrieved at the direction of, the parent or guardian who holds the account and who has consented on their child's behalf.

By creating an account you confirm that you are the parent or legal guardian of the child whose information you connect, and that you are entitled to access that child's school portal accounts.

If you believe a child has provided us information without a parent's involvement, email support@lucidlearner.com and we will delete it promptly.

Education records

SchoolBrief acts at your direction, as a parent exercising your own right of access to your child's education records. We are not a school official and we have no agreement with, or relationship to, your school district. We do not receive records from a school; we retrieve them using the access you already have, and we show them only back to you.

8. How Long We Keep It

9. Your Choices and Rights

  • Delete your account. Settings → Account → Delete account. This is immediate and permanent: it erases your account, every connected credential, your child's profile, and all stored academic data and briefs. There is nothing to email us for, and no waiting period.
  • Revoke Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Turn off notifications so no brief content leaves our servers.
  • Access or correct the information we hold, by asking us.

To exercise any right, or if a deletion does not work as described, contact support@lucidlearner.com.
