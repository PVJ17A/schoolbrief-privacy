SchoolBrief Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

SchoolBrief ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile application that helps parents and guardians monitor their child's school activity. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights. SchoolBrief is intended solely for use by parents and legal guardians.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your email address when you create an account.
  • Student portal credentials: Your Infinite Campus, ParentSquare, and/or EZSchoolPay usernames and passwords, stored encrypted in our secure vault and used solely to retrieve your child's school data on your behalf.
  • Student academic data: Assignments, grades, scores, and related school records retrieved from Infinite Campus.
  • School communications: Messages and announcements from ParentSquare, retrieved on your behalf.
  • Lunch balance data: Account balance information retrieved from EZSchoolPay on your behalf.
  • Child profile information: Your child's name and grade level, as provided by you.
  • Device push token: Used to send you daily brief notifications.
  • App usage data: Notification preferences and settings you configure in the app.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To log into Infinite Campus on your behalf and retrieve your child's academic data.
  • To generate your daily school brief and send push notifications.
  • To display historical briefs, grade trends, and weekly summaries in the app.
  • To alert you when a course grade falls below your chosen threshold.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a SOC 2 compliant cloud platform. Infinite Campus credentials are stored encrypted using Supabase Vault and are never stored in plain text. All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information or your child's academic data with any third parties, except as required by law. We use Supabase as our backend infrastructure provider, which processes data on our behalf under a data processing agreement.

5. Children's Privacy

SchoolBrief is intended for use by parents and legal guardians only. Children do not have accounts and do not interact with this app directly.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from minors. All child-related data stored in SchoolBrief — including a child's name, grade level, academic records, and school communications — is provided by and for the parent or guardian user who has consented to use the app on behalf of their child.

By creating an account, you represent that you are the parent or legal guardian of the child whose information is connected to your account, and that you have the authority to access that child's school portal data.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a minor without appropriate parental consent, please contact us immediately at support@lucidlearner.com and we will promptly delete it.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below. Upon deletion, all stored credentials, briefs, and academic data will be permanently removed within 30 days.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@lucidlearner.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email. Continued use of SchoolBrief after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
